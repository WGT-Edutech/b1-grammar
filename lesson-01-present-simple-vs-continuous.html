Present Simple vs Present Continuous

B1

Two ways to talk about now

We use Present Simple for habits, facts and routines. We use Present Continuous for things happening right now or around now.

Present Simple — habits and facts

Use it for things that happen regularly, permanent situations, or general truths.

I drink coffee every morning. (habit)
She works in Vienna. (permanent situation)
Water boils at 100°C. (fact)
Present Continuous — right now or temporary

Use it for actions in progress at this moment, or temporary situations.

I am drinking coffee right now. (in progress)
She is staying with her parents this week. (temporary)
Key signal words
  • Present Simple: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays, twice a year
  • Present Continuous: now, right now, at the moment, today, currently, this week
Be careful with state verbs

Some verbs describe a state, not an action. They are not normally used in continuous forms.

I know the answer. (NOT I am knowing)
She wants a coffee. (NOT She is wanting)
