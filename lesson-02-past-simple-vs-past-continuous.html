Past Simple vs Past Continuous

B1

Two ways to talk about the past

We use Past Simple for finished actions. We use Past Continuous for actions in progress at a specific moment in the past.

Past Simple — completed actions

Use it for single, finished events or habits in the past. It often comes with a specific time.

I finished work at 6 p.m. yesterday.
We visited Prague last summer.
Did you watch the match on Saturday?
Past Continuous — action in progress

Use it to describe something that was happening at a particular moment. It gives background to the story.

At 8 p.m. I was watching TV.
They were driving to Salzburg when it started to snow.
Interrupted actions

Use Past Continuous for the longer background action and Past Simple for the short interruption.

I was walking home when my phone rang.
She was cooking dinner when the lights went out.
Parallel actions

Use while or as with two actions happening at the same time.

While I was reading, my brother was playing video games.
As we were talking, the train arrived.
Signal words
  • Past Simple: yesterday, last week/month/year, ago, in 2020, when
  • Past Continuous: while, as, at 8 p.m., at that moment, all day
