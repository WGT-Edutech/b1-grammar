Past Simple vs Present Perfect

B1

Finished time or unfinished time?

Use Past Simple when the time is finished (yesterday, last year). Use Present Perfect when the time continues to now (today, this week, ever).

Past Simple + finished time

When you say exactly when something happened, use Past Simple.

I saw him yesterday.
Present Perfect + unfinished time

When the time period includes now, use Present Perfect.

I have seen him today.
Questions about experience

Use Present Perfect + "ever" to ask about life experience.

Have you ever been to Berlin?
Questions about a specific event

Use Past Simple when you ask about a specific moment.

Did you go to Berlin last summer?
Signal words
  • Past Simple: yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago, when.
  • Present Perfect: today, this week/month/year, ever, never, already, yet, just, since, for.

Exercise 2 — Correct the mistake

Each sentence has ONE mistake. Rewrite it correctly.

1 I have seen him last week. → I saw him last week
2 Did you ever eat Thai food? → Have you ever eaten Thai food?
3 She has been to Paris in 2019. → She went to Paris in 2019
4 We didn't finish yet. → We haven't finished yet
5 I have bought these shoes yesterday. → I bought these shoes yesterday
6 Have you watched the news this evening? (It is now late at night.) → Did you watch the news this evening?
7 He worked here since March. → He has worked here since March
8 They have visited us last Sunday. → They visited us last Sunday
